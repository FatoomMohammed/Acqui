How AcquiPredict predicts delay risk

A trained Random Forest Regressor estimates expected delay days for each case; the result is normalized into a 0-100 risk score and explained using the model's own learned feature importances.

🧠 Prediction pipeline

01Collect structured case factors + acquisition stage
02Encode into a feature vector (9 numeric + one-hot stage)
03Random Forest Regressor predicts expected delay in days
04Normalize prediction to a 0–100 risk score
05Classify Low / Medium / High and rank top risk drivers
